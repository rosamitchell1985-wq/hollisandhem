      Why shop with us

      The things you want to know before you buy

      • Free shipping over $75

        Standard delivery is $75.00 and up, or $6.95 flat. Expedited and overnight are available at checkout. Shipping policy.

      • 30-day returns

        Unworn items with tags attached can go back within 30 days. Refunds land 5–10 business days after we receive your return. Refund policy.

      • Transparent checkout

        Item prices exclude sales tax, which is calculated at checkout. Shipping is always shown as its own line before you pay. Nothing is added afterward.

      • Real people, fast replies

        Email support@hollisandhem.com or call (555) 010-4827. We reply within 1 business day.
