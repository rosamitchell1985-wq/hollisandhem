Cookie Policy

Effective date: January 15, 2026

This site stores two things in your browser: your shopping cart and your cookie-notice choice. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sent to us.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to keep, and which the browser sends back on later visits. Related technologies — local storage and session storage — also keep data in your browser, but do not send it anywhere automatically. This policy covers all of them, because from your point of view they raise the same question: what is being kept about me, and can I get rid of it?

2. What this site actually stores

Everything this site writes to your browser.
Name Type Purpose Expires
hollis-hem-cart-v1 Local storage Keeps the items in your shopping cart so they survive a page reload Until you clear it or empty your cart
hollis-hem-cookie-notice-v1 Local storage Remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice, so it does not reappear Until you clear your browser storage

That is the complete list. There are no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no social media pixels, no session replay, no fingerprinting and no cross-site identifiers on this site.

3. Cookie categories explained

  • Strictly necessary. Required for the site to function — remembering your cart, remembering your cookie choice. These cannot be switched off without breaking basic features. Both items in the table above fall in this category.
  • Functional. Remember preferences such as a chosen size or region. Not used on this site.
  • Analytics. Count visits and show which pages are used. Not used on this site. A live store would typically add one analytics provider, and this policy would be updated to name it.
  • Advertising and targeting. Build a profile to show you ads elsewhere. Not used on this site, and we do not intend to use them.

4. Third-party requests

The site loads two typefaces from Google Fonts. That request does not set a cookie, but it does reveal your IP address and browser details to Google, in the same way that loading any external resource would. If you prefer to avoid it, block third-party requests for fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com; the site remains fully readable using fonts already installed on your device.

Every image, stylesheet and script on this site is served from our own domain. Nothing else is loaded from anywhere else.

5. Your choice and the notice bar

The notice at the bottom of the page appears on your first visit and offers two buttons:

  • Essential only — records your choice and dismisses the notice. Because we only use strictly necessary storage, this changes nothing about what is stored.
  • Accept — records your choice and dismisses the notice.

Both choices are recorded in local storage so the notice does not follow you around. The notice is a bar at the bottom of the page and never blocks the content behind it.

If we ever add analytics or advertising storage, the notice will be changed to ask for consent before anything non-essential is set, and this page will be updated first.

6. How to control cookies in your browser

You can delete what this site has stored at any time. Clearing site data removes your cart, and the cookie notice will appear again on your next visit.

  • Chrome — Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies, or Settings → Privacy and security → Delete browsing data → Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari (macOS) — Safari → Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data. On iOS: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data.
  • Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data.
  • Edge — Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Private or incognito windows discard all of this automatically when you close them. In some private modes, storage is blocked entirely; this site is built to keep working in that case, though your cart will not survive a reload.

7. Global Privacy Control

If your browser or extension sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information. As explained in our Privacy Policy, we do not sell or share personal information in the first place.

8. Changes to this policy

If we add any new storage, we will update the table in section 2 and the effective date at the top of this page before the change goes live.
